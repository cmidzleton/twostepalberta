About this site

A lot of hall dances are scattered across Facebook pages, where they're easy to miss unless you already follow the right one. Small communities have a hard time getting the word out past their own area, and if you're not on social media you may never hear about them at all.

Our vision is for people who love social dancing to see what's on in other communities, and for dancing to grow across all ages and Alberta communities. Every listing links to the hall or organizer's own page so you can check it yourself.

We don't run any of these dances. This is just a list of hall dances people have posted online, gathered in one place. For tickets, changes or questions, go to the hall or whoever's putting it on.
