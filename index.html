About this site

Back to top

Finding out when a hall dance is on usually means knowing somebody, or spotting the poster at the post office after it's already happened. This puts them in one spot. Every listing links to the hall or organizer's own page so you can check it yourself.

We don't run any of these dances. This is just a list of hall dances people have posted online, gathered in one place. For tickets, changes or questions, go to the hall or whoever's putting it on.
