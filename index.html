Know of one we missed?

Add your hall's dance

Most small-town cabarets, New Year's Eve dances and Legion nights only get posted on Facebook or a poster at the post office. If you run one, or saw a poster, send it in and we'll list it free.

Email twostepalberta@gmail.com with:

  • Dance name, town and hall
  • Date and start time
  • Band and price
  • Who's hosting, and a phone number for questions
  • A photo of the poster, if you have one

About

Keeping the hall dance going

Hall dances keep small towns together, and plenty of them are run by volunteers who are getting ready to retire. Two-Step Alberta puts the dates in one place so more people show up, the band gets paid, and the hall stays open. Every listing links to where we found it.
